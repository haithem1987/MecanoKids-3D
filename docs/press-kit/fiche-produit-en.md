# Product Sheet — Octo Gears

## The 3D Mechanical Workshop for Kids Ages 5–12

---

### In one sentence

Octo Gears is an educational 3D game where children assemble mechanical vehicles — cars, planes, construction machines — and discover how and why they work.

---

### Audience

| Profile | Age | Mode |
|---|---|---|
| Guided discovery | 5–7 | Voice instructions, wide snap radius, no reading required |
| Autonomous exploration | 8–10 | Progressive hints, transfer challenges |
| Open challenges | 10–12 | Free mode, fault-finding diagnostic |

---

### What children do

1. **Pick a mission** in one of 5 worlds (Road, Sea, Air, Construction, Rail)
2. **Assemble the vehicle** part by part in 3D (tap or drag-and-drop)
3. **See it come to life**: wheels spin, propellers turn, gears mesh
4. **Hear the explanation**: a voice links what just happened to a concept (power transmission, gears, levers…)
5. **Take the transfer challenge**: find the broken part by listening and observing

---

### Content (v1.0)

- **15 missions** across 15 vehicles
- **5 worlds**: Road & City, Sea & Sailing, Air & Space, Construction, Rail & Mines
- **12 printable lesson plans** for primary school (ages 7–11)
- Aligned with science and technology curricula (UK KS2, French cycle 2-3, UAE STEM framework)
